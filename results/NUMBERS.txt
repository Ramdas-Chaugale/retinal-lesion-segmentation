The mask-building step applies two corrective rules. Both were prompted by
defects in the delivered annotations, and each rule fires on exactly one image.

**Open outline.** One drawn stroke has a gap, so `findContours` returns an open
curve and filling it adds nothing: the stored mask was the 2,255-pixel stroke
with the lesion interior unlabelled. 99.5% of its occupied columns held two runs

### Soft erosion penalty against fragment count
| Fragments | Kernel 5 (256) | increase | Kernel 9 (native) | increase |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | 0.020508 | - | 0.039714 | - |
| 2 | 0.021810 | +6.3% | 0.041178 | +3.7% |
| 3 | 0.023112 | +12.7% | 0.042643 | +7.4% |
| 5 | 0.025716 | +25.4% | 0.045573 | +14.8% |

The kernel-9 baseline is nearly double the kernel-5 baseline because a larger kernel erodes more
of the main blob. Fragments are therefore a smaller share of the signal at native, which is a
mechanism for why the penalty is weaker there.

### Why the penalty is too weak, the arithmetic
Verified 2026-08-31 by rerunning the synthetic shapes. Frame **24,576 px** (128 x 192), main blob
4,000 px, each added fragment 6 x 6 = 36 px.

| Quantity | Kernel 5 | Kernel 9 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Erosion shell of the main blob | **504 px** | **976 px** |
| Baseline penalty | 0.020508 | 0.039714 |
| Rise per added fragment | **0.001302** | **0.001465** |
| That rise as a share of baseline | 6.3% | 3.7% |

At kernel 5 a 36 px fragment keeps 4 px and so adds 32; at kernel 9 it is erased outright and adds
all 36. **Being erased completely is the largest contribution a fragment can make**, so a fragment
smaller than the kernel costs the most, not the least. The share nonetheless falls as the kernel
grows, because the shell it is measured against grows faster.

Against the whole loss, at kernel 5:

| Term | Value |
|------|-------|
| Rise per fragment | 0.001302 |
| Erosion weight | 0.2 |
| **Cost of a fragment** | **0.00026** |
| Dice gap on the image examined | 0.028 |
| Dice + BCE weight | 0.8 |
| **Value of the overlap** | **0.0224** |
| **Ratio** | **about 86 to 1** |

The overlap term outweighs the connectivity term by about 86 to 1, so the model accepts a second
component whenever that buys any overlap at all.

### Erosion applied to a real fragmented prediction
256, Modified OCT-F 2ch on scan_02: 3 components before, **2 after**.
Native, Modified OCT-F 2ch on scan_03: 2 components before, **2 after**, the fragment is
wider than the 9x9 kernel and survives unchanged.

### Why a blob count cannot be a loss
`cv2.connectedComponents` returns a plain float (26 components in the demo), not a tensor, so
`.backward()` cannot be called. Soft erosion returns a tensor (loss 0.489525) and the gradient
reaches the input (magnitude 0.000468).

### Compactness, three synthetic shapes
Circle, area 3,853, compactness **1.67**. Elongated lesion, area 2,880, **2.29**. Fragmented
lesion, area 2,592, **2.99**. Fragmentation scores worst, but an ordinary elongated lesion is
already penalised most of the way there.
